Mat Pilates Classes

Build strength, improve flexibility, and find your flow through intentional movement in a welcoming studio environment.

Movement That Meets You Where You Are

At Form & Flow Pilates, our mat classes focus on controlled movements, mindful breathing, core strength, and proper alignment. Whether you are looking for a gentle reset or a more challenging workout, there is a class for you.

All Levels

A full-body workout centered on core stability, posture, balance, and controlled movement. A great way to build a consistent Pilates practice.

Gentle Flow

Power Pilates

A more challenging mat session that combines longer holds, precise repetitions, and dynamic sequences to build strength and endurance.

A restorative session that emphasizes flexibility, breathing, gentle core activation, and relaxation. Perfect for a reset after a busy day.
