Mat Pilates Classes in Iowa City, IA

Discover a mindful way to move with mat Pilates classes focused on core stability, flexibility, posture, and full-body strength. Find a class style that matches your experience and goals.

Find the class that fits your day

At Form & Flow Pilates, our fictional class offerings are designed to make movement feel approachable and rewarding. Mat Pilates uses your body weight and a floor mat rather than large exercise machines. Exercises combine controlled movement with breathing and attention to alignment. A mat, comfortable clothing, and a willingness to learn are all you need to get started.

Full-Body Flow

What are the benefits of mat Pilates?

A consistent Pilates practice can help develop core strength, balance, flexibility, coordination, and awareness of posture. The controlled pace encourages you to pay attention to how you move instead of rushing through repetitions. Results vary by individual, and Pilates can complement other forms of physical activity.

Prepare for your first class

Wear comfortable clothing that lets you move freely and bring water. Arrive with an open mind, take breaks when needed, and tell your instructor about movement concerns before class. Exercises can often be adjusted to suit different experience levels. If you are unsure whether a movement is appropriate for you, ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
